# Licenses and upstream source

LingoTweaker-WASM is distributed under LGPL-2.1-or-later. The bundled language data retain their original component licenses. The download and the repository contain the corresponding notices, provenance and build references. [LingoTweaker and data notices](engine/vendor/SOURCE.md)

The Local Grammar Pro application code and icons have no separate license declaration. The maintainer reports that the project was generated with Grok/GPT; ownership and provenance have not been confirmed. AI generation does not establish third-party rights clearance. A license for these parts requires a decision by a person entitled to grant it.

The upstream notices explicitly retain provenance/license evidence gaps for parts of the English language data. Upstream's recorded acceptance of those gaps is not a grant of rights by the original rights holders. Keeping notices alone does not resolve missing redistribution rights or all source-distribution obligations. [Distribution status and unresolved items](DISTRIBUTION.md)
